# Step 08: Organization

Goal: an organization's owner runs it from the web (`features.md`: members, library members and roles, namespaces, quotas, its OIDC provider, all V1): its name, members and invitations, who reaches which library, which libraries dedup together, its own login provider, its ownership and its deletion; any account creates a company organization; every member sees how full it is.

## `core`: nothing missing but one flag

The gaps listed in CLAUDE.md (library members, namespaces, creating and renaming organizations) were closed by earlier `core` releases: `POST /organizations`, `PATCH /organizations/{id}`, `/organizations/{id}/members`, `/owner`, `/libraries/{id}/members/{user}` (`read`, `write`), `/organizations/{id}/namespaces`, `/namespaces/{id}/libraries/{library}`, `/organizations/{id}/identity-providers`, `DELETE /organizations/{id}`. CLAUDE.md's line goes.

Missing: **whether this account may create an organization** (the setting `organization_creation`: `anyone` or `admins`). Without it the app offers a button the server refuses. Added: `Me.can_create_organizations`. Not in `GET /instance`: no other setting is public (decisions.md), and it's about this account anyway (an instance admin always may). A `core` minor release, pinned here.

## Where

- **Usage, for every member**: on the organization's page (`/<org>`, its libraries), a line above them: "1.2 GiB of 10 GiB used", a bar when there's a quota (`GET /organizations/{id}`: `used`, `quota`). The personal organization ("My files") too.
- **The owner's page**: `/<org>/settings`, from a gear next to the organization's name in the sidebar (owners only), and a link on `/<org>`. **One page in sections**, as the account's (short sections, found with the browser's search). A member who isn't the owner never sees it (`/<org>/settings` answers the 404 page). The personal organization has none (no members, no provider, deleted with the account): its gear doesn't show.
- **A new organization**: "New organization" at the foot of the sidebar's organizations (when `can_create_organizations`): a name, then its settings page.

## The owner's page, in order

1. **Name**: renamed in place (a company organization).
2. **Members**: each one's email, joined or invited (pending), the owner marked. **Add** by email (an account joins at once and is told by email; another address gets an invitation: the server decides, the toast says which). **Remove** a member or cancel an invitation, confirmed (its library access goes with it). Never the owner.
3. **Libraries**: every library of the organization (`GET /organizations/{id}/libraries`, the owner's included or not), each with its member count. **Members** opens a dialog: who reaches it and how (`read`, `write`), a role changed in place, removed, or added from the organization's members (invited ones too: their access is ready when they accept). A library with no members is said ("nobody reaches it"). Only here: not in the sidebar's library menu (sharing is the owner's job, in one place).
4. **Library groups** (the API's dedup namespaces; reviewed together in the browser once built, before anything else is settled). Named for the user, with what they do said under the heading: "Libraries in a group store identical files only once. Their members can then find out whether a file exists in the group's other libraries." Not "grouping" alone: it reads as tidying, and an owner would group "All staff" with "HR" for neatness. Each group: its name (renamed in place), its libraries by name, each removable (×: at once, the trust revoked, nothing re-uploaded); **Add a library** among the others, confirmed with the warning naming them ("Members of Payroll and of All staff will be able to tell whether a file exists in the other library"). **Create** (a name), **delete** (confirmed: its libraries stop deduplicating together). Empty: when to use one ("Group libraries whose members all trust each other, such as a team's projects").
5. **Login provider** (its OIDC provider: one in practice, the API allows several): name, issuer, client ID, client secret (written only: never shown back), enabled. Shown for the provider's setup: the **callback URL** to register there (`<origin>/api/v1/auth/oidc/<id>/callback`), and the **login link** to share with members (`<origin>/api/v1/auth/oidc/<id>/start`: the login page lists only instance-wide providers, decisions.md). Its sessions are restricted to the organization (said there). Delete, confirmed: the identities linked through it go too.
6. **Ownership**: give the organization to a joined member, confirmed; this page then closes for you.
7. **Delete the organization**: every library, file and version **at once, no trash**: confirmed by typing its name, shown in the dialog to copy (unlike a library, which goes to the trash).

## Tests

Playwright, against the real server: an organization created from the sidebar, renamed; a member invited (Mailpit's link), accepted, then given `read` on a library and seeing it read-only, then `write`; a namespace created, a library added through the warning, removed; the provider created (its callback URL and login link shown; no IdP in the tests), deleted; the ownership given (the settings gone for the old owner); the organization deleted (its name typed), gone from the sidebar. A member who isn't the owner gets no gear and a 404 on `/<org>/settings`. The usage line shown with a quota (set through the admin API).
